About Bandre's Legal

Commercial insight. Human-centred legal service.

Bandre Legal is a full-service law firm based in Lagos, Nigeria, committed to delivering innovative, commercially driven, and client-centred legal solutions to individuals, entrepreneurs, startups, investors, businesses, corporations, and institutions. We provide strategic legal support that protects our clients' interests, facilitates business growth, manages risk, and creates lasting value.

We combine legal expertise with sound commercial judgment to navigate complex legal, regulatory, transactional, and dispute resolution challenges, enabling our clients to make informed decisions and pursue their objectives with confidence.

  • Business and governance advisory
  • Risk-aware dispute management
  • Transparent communication and execution
